About TrashToTech

A capstone project for Barangay 39, Calvary Hill, Tacloban City

Insert a plastic bottle, earn points based on how many you deposit, and redeem those points for WiFi time or a voucher. Built to reduce plastic waste while giving residents free internet access.

Privacy Policy

What this machine collects

This machine assigns your device an anonymous ID to track your points and WiFi sessions. No personal information is collected unless you voluntarily sign up. Data is used only to operate the rewards system for this project.
