TrashToTech

A plastic-bottle-to-WiFi reverse vending system, developed as a capstone project for Barangay 39, Calvary Hill, Tacloban City.

Bottles are counted and weighed at the machine, converted into points, and redeemable for WiFi time or vouchers. This dashboard manages users, transactions, rewards, rates, and system logs.
